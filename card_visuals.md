# Fark — Card Art Concepts (all 133) · abstract tarot

Abstract object-metaphors (no creatures), cosy-but-not-childish, pixel-tarot, patterned grounds, no internal frame. Anchored by THE PYRE; reworked motion-designer + game-dev.

## 1. THE FLINTLOCK  ·  `flintlock`
- **Visual:** Flint struck against steel at the single instant of ignition, throwing ONE oversized white-gold spark-star that fills the frame — no flame yet, just that lone brilliant burst. One strike, one chance, the whole turn's luck hanging on a single spark.
- **Composition:** Flint and steel tucked small at the lower-left corner; the solitary spark blazes huge and off-center against deep ember-dark negative space so it reads as one shape.
- **Palette:** Slate-grey flint, gunmetal steel, one searing white-gold spark, deep ember-amber ground.
- **Hook:** The lone white-gold spark-star against the dark — one unmistakable burst, legible even at thumbnail size.

## 2. COPPER PINCHER  ·  `copper_pincher`
- **Visual:** Two fat copper coins pinched edge-on in the jaws of a simple wooden coin-clamp, squeezed so hard they nearly ring — one stamped a bold '1', the other a bold '5', both catching the light. Thrift rendered as a single tight, careful object.
- **Composition:** Centered clamp filling the frame edge-to-edge; the two coins large and frontal so the stamped 1 and 5 read instantly.
- **Palette:** Warm copper-orange, dark walnut wood, brass-yellow highlights, soft cream backdrop.
- **Hook:** Two plump coins gripped in a vice, stamped 1 and 5 — reads at a glance as careful, counted money.

## 3. HALF MEASURE  ·  `half_measure`
- **Visual:** A pewter tankard filled to exactly its etched halfway line and stopped there on purpose, with THREE small bone dice settled on the surface where foam would be. Restraint poured into a cup — three dice, never one more.
- **Composition:** Single tankard centered and cropped at the rim; the bold horizontal half-line slices the frame cleanly in two, three dice clustered above it.
- **Palette:** Cool pewter-silver, honey-amber ale, ivory-bone dice, muted sage backdrop.
- **Hook:** The stark half-full line splitting the cup with exactly three dice floating — one clean horizon, one clear count.

## 4. THE HEARTH  ·  `the_hearth`
- **Visual:** A snug chunky-pixel flame glowing safe inside a stone hearth arch, warm firelight pooling out onto the floor below — the touchstone Pyre, sheltered and homely, banked behind warm stone.
- **Composition:** Centered stone arch framing the fire; the flame fills the opening edge-to-edge, firelight pooling warm along the bottom edge.
- **Palette:** Tangerine-and-gold flame, dusky stone-grey arch, deep maroon shadow, cream firelight.
- **Hook:** A vibrant flame cupped inside a stone arch — the warmest, most inviting, most sheltered shape in the deck.

## 5. SLOW BURN  ·  `slow_burn`
- **Visual:** A tall taper candle burned down in three even bands, each notch ringed with a thin gold band, one steady flame still strong at the top. Patience measured in three equal thirds of melting wax — every third notch pays out.
- **Composition:** Vertical candle off-center, edge-to-edge top to bottom; the three gold notch-rings stack as a calm rhythm, flame cropped bright at the very top.
- **Palette:** Beeswax-cream wax, soft gold notch-rings, one orange flame, deep indigo night.
- **Hook:** A tall candle banded into clear thirds with one bright flame — a slow, calm vertical climb you can count.

## 6. FOOL'S GOLD  ·  `fools_gold`
- **Visual:** A single cubic chunk of pyrite resting on a small plum-velvet cushion — faceted and gleaming gold, but unmistakably a hard ore-cube, not a coin — throwing one sharp, true white glint. Fake treasure that still pays a lucky bonus.
- **Composition:** Centered cube filling the frame at a three-quarter angle, a sharp white glint-star pinned to its top facet.
- **Palette:** Brassy fool's-gold yellow, cool steel-grey facets, plum velvet cushion, white glint-spark.
- **Hook:** A blocky golden cube with one sharp glint — looks like treasure, sits squarely like a die.

## 7. MILLER'S TOLL  ·  `millers_toll`
- **Visual:** A round grey millstone seen from above with THREE small burlap grain-sacks set on top as the miller's cut, a thin stream of gold flour spilling off the stone's edge into a waiting measure below. A tidy tithe taken from every turn of the wheel.
- **Composition:** Centered millstone slightly from above, three sacks clustered on top, the gold flour-stream falling off the lower-right edge into the measure.
- **Palette:** Dusty stone-grey wheel, wheat-gold flour, burlap-tan sacks, warm ochre backdrop.
- **Hook:** The round grey stone with three little sacks and a gold spill — a counted tithe read in an instant.

## 8. THE SCAVENGER  ·  `scavenger`
- **Visual:** Two mismatched found coins — one stamped a bold '1', one stamped a bold '5' — lashed together with a single twine knot into one lucky pairing. Scraps made whole by knotting them into a pair.
- **Composition:** Centered close-up of the two overlapping coins, the bold twine knot dead-center binding them into one unit.
- **Palette:** Tarnished bronze and bright silver coins, jute-brown twine, deep teal ground.
- **Hook:** Two mismatched coins lashed with one knot — instantly reads as 'one plus five, bound together'.

## 9. THE LAST STAND  ·  `last_stand`
- **Visual:** A single bone die balanced upright on one corner at the very lip of a table-cliff, showing a bold 1, holding its ground against the drop — the final lone roll, defiant and precarious. Tense and poetic, never grim.
- **Composition:** Die off-center on the lower-right table-edge, vast glowing sunset-amber sky-space above to make the lonely stand pop as a silhouette.
- **Palette:** Bone-ivory die, dark oak table-edge, one black pip, glowing sunset-amber sky.
- **Hook:** One lone die balanced on its corner at the cliff-edge — a precarious silhouette that pops against open sky.

## 10. THE MARTYR  ·  `martyr`
- **Visual:** A guttered-out votive candle trailing one curling thread of grey smoke, and riding that smoke a single bright ember drifting up to relight a fresh wick beside it. One sacrifice buys a second flame — gentle, hopeful, a survived fall.
- **Composition:** Two candles linked by one diagonal curl: spent candle lower-left, fresh candle upper-right, the smoke-and-ember the single line joining them.
- **Palette:** Ash-grey smoke, warm wax-ivory, one ember-orange spark, deep midnight-blue ground.
- **Hook:** A wisp of smoke carrying one ember to a new wick — quiet rebirth read in a single curling line.

## 11. BLOOD DEBT  ·  `blood_debt`
- **Visual:** A set of brass scales: the low pan weighted by a single crimson wax debt-seal, the high pan rising as it fills with gold coins — the debt turning to profit as others fall. A ledger settled, shown as a tipping balance with no harm at all.
- **Composition:** Centered scales cropped close on the beam; the crimson-sealed pan sits low-left, the gold-coin pan lifts up to the right.
- **Palette:** Antique brass scales, one deep crimson wax seal, warm gold coins, charcoal ground.
- **Hook:** A tilted brass scale — one red seal against a rising heap of gold — debt versus payback in one clear shape.

## 12. LEAKY CUP  ·  `leaky_cup`

- **Composition:** Cracked cup tipping from the upper-left, five dice streaming diagonally down toward the empty cup at the lower-right edge.
- **Palette:** Worn leather-brown cup, ivory-bone dice, one dark crack-line, warm tavern-gold ground.
- **Hook:** Dice trickling from a cracked cup toward another — fortune draining to a rival, read in an instant.

## 13. CROW'S LUCK  ·  `crows_luck`
- **Visual:** A four-leaf clover pinned flat under one heavy jet-black feather laid across it on the diagonal — and the topmost leaf is snapped clean off, its bare broken stem still quivering in the gap. A whole run of luck with one piece plucked away under the feather's weight.
- **Composition:** Off-center close-up: clover anchored lower-left filling a third of the frame, the feather slicing corner-to-corner. The empty notch where the missing leaf was sits dead at the crossing point, drawing the eye to the gap.
- **Palette:** Ink-black feather, vivid emerald clover, one bruised-amber snapped stem, bright parchment-cream ground.
- **Hook:** The black feather pinning a clover that's missing one leaf — a perfect run with a piece torn out.

## 14. THE TAB  ·  `the_tab`
- **Visual:** A fat little leather coin-pouch cinched shut and locked under a bold crimson wax seal stamped with a single chalk tally-stroke — the coins are owed, sealed, and held back a while. A looped cord knots it tight.
- **Composition:** Centered, cropped tight: the sealed pouch fills the whole frame, the round crimson seal punched dead-center and catching a sharp overhead glint. The cord-loop runs off the bottom edge.
- **Palette:** Oxblood leather, bright crimson wax seal, stark chalk-white tally-stroke, cool pewter cord.
- **Hook:** A crimson wax seal with one tally scratched in — a debt locked under seal, waiting to clear.

## 15. TAXING BREATH  ·  `taxing_breath`

- **Composition:** Off-center vertical: the funnel runs upper-left to lower-right, its wide mouth cropped off the top edge. The single siphoned coin caught mid-air at the side-spout is the focal pop, isolated against dark ground.
- **Palette:** Warm brass funnel, bright gold coin-stream, deep bronze spout, dark teal shadow ground.
- **Hook:** One gold coin peeling off through a side-spout — a quiet tax skimmed from every breath that passes.

## 16. THIEF'S LUCK  ·  `thieves_luck`

- **Composition:** Centered-low close-up: the lantern fills the lower frame, the single rising coin drawing the eye up into the dark space above. A soft golden halo rings the open door.
- **Palette:** Honey lantern-glow, brass frame, one bright gold coin, deep midnight-blue surround.
- **Hook:** A single coin lifted out of an open lantern door — a small, lucky pilfer in the lamplight.

## 17. PLAGUE WIND  ·  `plague_wind`

- **Composition:** Off-center: the cowering flame lower-right, the pewter guard curving boldly in from the right edge to shelter it. Crisp diagonal wind-lines rake from upper-left. Cropped tight on the small bent flame.
- **Palette:** Pale guttering amber flame, cold slate-blue wind-lines, dull pewter guard, deep muted-sage ground.
- **Hook:** A tiny flame cowering behind a cupped guard against the wind — caution made visible.

## 18. KING'S RANSOM  ·  `kings_ransom`

## 19. SOOTY TABLE  ·  `sooty_table`

- **Palette:** Charcoal soot, warm bone-white die, faded grey pips on its sides, dull ember-orange table-glow.

## 20. THRIFTY GAMBLER  ·  `thrifty_gambler`

- **Composition:** Centered, near-symmetrical: the scale beam fills the mid-frame, its two pans cropped at the left and right edges. Tight close-up on the level fulcrum, the dead-level beam reading instantly.

## 21. THE IRON CROWN  ·  `iron_crown`

- **Composition:** Edge-to-edge horizontal: the dark crown band crosses the lower frame, the dice-spires ascending like a staircase toward the upper-right, the tallest cropped at the top edge.
- **Palette:** Dark iron band, glowing forge-orange dice-points, bright gold sparks, deep charcoal ground.
- **Hook:** Dice mounting like a staircase of glowing spires — a crown that grows taller with every hot streak.

## 22. JESTER'S MASK  ·  `jesters_mask`
- **Visual:** A jester's belled cap tumbling mid-air, its three points splayed wild and its bells flung loose, dice spilling out of it every which way — tidy order shaken into delightful nonsense. Playful, never sinister.
- **Composition:** Off-center dynamic diagonal: the cap tumbling from upper-left to lower-right, bells and dice scattering off the edges. Cropped close so the burst of motion breaks the frame.
- **Palette:** Harlequin purple-and-gold cap, brass bells, scattering ivory dice, warm plum ground.

## 23. SERPENT'S WHISPER  ·  `serpents_whisper`

- **Palette:** Hemp-brown rope, one cool jade knot-shadow, warm gold coin-bag, dusky indigo ground.

- **Composition:** Centered: the brass lens and its three sharp, still dice fill the core of the frame, the blurred reroll-dice streaking off the corners. Close-up with shallow focus making the chosen three pop.
- **Palette:** Polished brass lens-ring, clear ivory chosen dice, soft motion-blur grey, warm amber lamplight ground.
- **Hook:** Three crisp dice held still under glass while the rest blur away — the gambler's choosing eye.

## 25. GROG'S FLASK  ·  `grogs_flask`
