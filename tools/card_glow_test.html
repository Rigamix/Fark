    How it works
    
Two divs sit behind the card, filled with solid colour and masked by the card PNG's alpha. The art on top hides them completely — all you ever see is their drop-shadow, which spreads outward from the real silhouette at constant width, so the halo hugs rounded corners and shield shapes.

    
Brightness past 1.0 stops raising opacity and starts applying brightness() instead, which burns the colour toward white. That's how you get a hot core without washing out the bloom.
