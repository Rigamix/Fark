Hot Dice
All 6 dice score in a single roll — re-roll all 6 and keep your turn points. A full straight always triggers Hot Dice.
On your turn
Roll all dice. Select at least one scoring die. Then choose to BANK your points or ROLL again with remaining dice.
Bust
If no dice score after a roll, you bust — all turn points are lost and your turn ends immediately.
Winning
First to reach the target score wins the match. The target varies by rung on the ladder.
Edicts
Higher rungs impose edicts — special constraints on your turn. Read the edict banner carefully before rolling.
Dice types
Different dice have different face biases. Heavier dice roll more scoring faces but cost more gold at the Blacksmith. Your 6-die loadout is set before each match.
Your hand
You earn your first card after winning your first match. Each card gives a passive bonus during play.
Wagering
At higher rungs, both you and your rival stake one card. Win and you take theirs. Lose and yours is gone.
Tarnish
Cards gain a tarnish mark each time they help you win. At 3 marks they wear out and stop working until cleaned at the Blacksmith.
Rarities
Tin — starter cards, common effects Silver — stronger effects, found at higher rungs Gold — rare and powerful, high-rung rewards
Ruined
No cards left to wager when required — you are Ruined. Grind early rungs for gold or start fresh.
