# Patron growth dialogue — band-4, band-7, and recognition lines

23 patrons, three lines each: a band-4 backstory addition, a band-7
addition, and one growth-acknowledgment line for the rare "first
meeting since their band changed" beat. Same mundane, concrete voice
standard as the earlier pass — small, real signs of change (a new
customer, an apprentice, a habit shifting), never a reflective
statement about growth itself.

Six patrons found later in the build (Twill, Fenn, Ferrand, Odo,
Ollis, Tam) and Peck aren't included — same reason as the original
voice pass: not enough grounding to write them without risking
inventing a voice rather than extending one.
