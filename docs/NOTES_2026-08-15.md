## PRESERVE'S KEPT DIE IS OPEN - and the diagnosis is complete

Denis: 'it becomes small and grey at the bottom, when I roll it scales
slightly but stays grey, doesn't move back to its lane or full colour,
I can't select it, and its lane must not be used by another die.'

DIAGNOSED, root cause: the payout MINTS A DECORATIVE CHIP. startPTurn
builds the preserved die with mkDie into #keptRow with class `in-tray` -
that class IS the small dim tray styling, which is the 'small and grey'.
Worse, under the 3D layer refreshKeptTray returns early and kept dice
live on the THROW LINE, so a chip in #keptRow is on a surface the game
no longer uses: it has no lane, no pool entry, no click handler - hence
unselectable - and P734's park/return moved a thing that was never in
the right place to begin with. And _dropLanes(1) only shrinks the lane
COUNT; nothing reserves the preserved die's own lane, so the next deal
can hand it to another die.

THE FIX, at the root (do NOT patch the chip): the preserved die should
come back as a REAL POOL DIE in its own lane, flagged as already
scored - the same object every other die on the throw line is - rather
than a decoration in a retired tray. Concretely: at the payout, insert a
pool entry at G._pvLane carrying {val,mat,ench} with committed=true and
a `preserved` flag, let the normal deal skip that lane (the walk already
reads _pvLane - P691 built the record and P726 finally captured it), and
give it the amber shell through the same D3X.amberShell the cast uses.
Then it is full size, in colour, in its lane, and the lane cannot be
taken because a die is genuinely in it. P734's park/return animation can
ride on the pool die unchanged.

## THE SETTLE DRAG IS OPEN (P736 reverted by P740)

Denis has reported it twice: dice hang, then slide slowly into place 'as
if against an invisible wall'. MEASURED: tapes run 1.2-1.9s and 183-650ms
of that is crawl (per-die frames displacing 0.004-0.05, i.e. 0.24-3.0
units/s). P736 tried two fixes - release the lane pull inside the lane
radius, and zero a die after six frames in a creep band. BOTH FAILED:
the release meant dice were never centred (they bundled - Denis saw it
immediately), and the SEEDED A/B (tools/_probe_settle_ab.js: a
deterministic Math.random inside the frame, so both arms solve identical
throws; toggle via creepFrames/laneRadius, no new code) put the drag
WORSE - 573ms vs 498ms. Reverted whole.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE: the loop ends on the STILL TEST - 14 consecutive
frames under stillEps (0.006 displacement, i.e. 0.36 u/s) - and the tape
cut trims trailing frames by the same epsilon. So every frame between
'visibly stopped' and 'under 0.36 u/s for 14 frames' is recorded and
played back. That band IS the crawl. UNTRIED IDEAS, cheapest first:
(1) cut the tape at a LARGER epsilon than the sim's still test - the
physics is unchanged, the tail simply is not shown; measure the position
jump the cut introduces and keep it under a few % of a die width;
(2) shorten the tail by raising damping only after N still-ish frames;
(3) play the last stretch of tape at >1x. The A/B harness makes any of
them a 10-minute measurement - USE IT before shipping, this fix has
failed twice on eye alone.
